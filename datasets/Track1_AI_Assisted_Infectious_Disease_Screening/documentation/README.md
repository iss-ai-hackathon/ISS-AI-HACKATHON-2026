# Track 1: AI-Assisted Infectious Disease Screening

## Overview

Welcome to **Track 1: AI-Assisted Infectious Disease Screening** of the **ISS 2026 AI Hackathon**.

This track challenges participants to develop Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) solutions that assist in the early screening of infectious diseases using synthetic patient-level clinical data. The dataset has been developed to simulate common infectious disease presentations in Nigeria while preserving patient privacy.

---

## Challenge Statement

Develop an AI model capable of predicting the most likely infectious disease based on demographic information, clinical symptoms, vital signs, laboratory findings, and epidemiological risk factors.

Solutions should demonstrate good predictive performance while remaining clinically interpretable and suitable for resource-limited healthcare settings.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains synthetic patient records with variables including:

* Patient demographics
* Clinical symptoms
* Vital signs
* Laboratory investigations
* Epidemiological risk factors
* Environmental variables (where applicable)
* Target diagnosis

### Diseases Included

* Malaria
* Typhoid Fever
* Tuberculosis
* Cholera
* Lassa Fever
* COVID-19
* Acute Respiratory Infection
* Meningitis
* Healthy Controls

## Suggested Workflow

1. Explore the dataset.
2. Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
3. Engineer relevant predictive features.
4. Train one or more AI/ML models.
5. Evaluate model performance.
6. Interpret model predictions.
7. Present findings.
