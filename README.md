# ISS 2026 AI Hackathon

## Artificial Intelligence for Public Health Innovation

Welcome to the official repository for the **ISS 2026 AI Hackathon**. This repository contains the datasets, documentation, starter resources, and supporting materials for all three competition tracks.

The hackathon aims to bring together students, researchers, software developers, clinicians, public health professionals, and AI enthusiasts to develop innovative artificial intelligence solutions that address pressing healthcare and public health challenges in Nigeria.

---

# Objectives

The ISS 2026 AI Hackathon seeks to:

* Promote the application of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare and public health.
* Encourage multidisciplinary collaboration.
* Develop innovative and scalable AI solutions for real-world health challenges.
* Strengthen participants' skills in data science, machine learning, natural language processing, and geospatial analytics.
* Foster open science and reproducible AI research.

---

# Competition Tracks

## Track 1: AI-Assisted Infectious Disease Screening

Develop AI and Machine Learning models capable of assisting healthcare workers in the early screening of infectious diseases using structured patient-level clinical data.

### Dataset Provided

* Synthetic Nigerian infectious disease dataset
* Data dictionary
* Problem statement
* Evaluation criteria
* Baseline resources

Example applications include:

* Disease prediction
* Clinical decision support
* Risk stratification
* Early disease screening

---

## Track 2: AI-Assisted Clinical Documentation

Develop Natural Language Processing (NLP) solutions capable of transforming doctor–patient conversations into structured clinical documentation.

### Benchmark Dataset

**NaClinDoc-500 (Nigeria Clinical Documentation Benchmark)**

The benchmark includes:

* Clinical consultation transcripts
* Patient metadata
* Encounter metadata
* SOAP notes
* ICD-10 diagnosis codes
* Investigation recommendations
* Treatment plans

Example applications include:

* SOAP note generation
* Clinical summarization
* Diagnosis prediction
* ICD-10 coding
* Information extraction

---

## Track 3: AI for Climate and Environmental Health

Develop AI solutions that integrate climate, environmental, geospatial, and demographic data to address climate-sensitive public health challenges.

Participants will work with publicly available datasets from official sources, including:

* NASA POWER
* CHIRPS
* WorldPop
* OpenStreetMap
* SRTM Digital Elevation Model
* MODIS NDVI (optional)

Example applications include:

* Malaria risk prediction
* Cholera hotspot prediction
* Flood vulnerability assessment
* Heat stress prediction
* Environmental health surveillance

# Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Navigate to the folder corresponding to your selected competition track.
3. Read the track-specific documentation.
4. Review the problem statement and evaluation criteria.
5. Download or access the required datasets.
6. Develop, evaluate, and document your AI solution.
7. Submit your project before the submission deadline.

---

# Submission Requirements

Each team must submit:

* Public GitHub Repository
* Technical Report (Maximum 5 pages)
* Presentation Slides
* Demonstration Video (Maximum 5 minutes)

Submission instructions will be communicated by the organizing committee.

---

# Evaluation

Each track has its own evaluation rubric.

Participants should review the following documents before starting:

* Problem Statement
* Evaluation Criteria
* README
* Data Dictionary
* Data Acquisition Guide (Track 3)

---

# Rules

* All submitted work must be original.
* Teams must comply with the licensing requirements of any external datasets used.
* All preprocessing and modeling steps should be documented.
* Solutions should be reproducible.
* Participants are encouraged to use explainable AI techniques where appropriate.
